      About

      NC Idea Challenge winner

      Opportunity Unmuted is an ongoing podcast series made by students. Each short episode points you to free programs, scholarships, tutoring, and mental health support that already exist in our school and community.

      Created by Harini Jayakumar, South Iredell High School, Mooresville, NC.

      Found us on a poster?

      We put QR code posters in schools, libraries, and community spaces. Scan one any time to jump straight to the newest episodes or to help.

      Put the app on your phone

      On iPhone, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". On Android, tap the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". After that it opens like any other app, even offline.
